Whooshy – Slide the Arrows Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Kiran Kotla Studios ("we", "us", or "our") develops Whooshy – Slide the Arrows (the "App"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information stored on your device

The App stores game progress and settings locally on your device. We do not operate user accounts and do not ask you to provide your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Advertising

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide optional in-game advertising. The App requests child-directed treatment and a general audience content rating. Google may process information such as device, app, network, and ad interaction data to provide, measure, and protect its advertising services. Google's processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is handled by Google under its own policies and applicable agreements.

Children's privacy

The App is designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising requests are configured for child-directed treatment.

Permissions

The App may use vibration for haptic feedback and network access for advertising services. It does not request access to contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's features or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to kiran.kotla90@gmail.com.
